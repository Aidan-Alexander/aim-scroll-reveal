More filler, so the page has somewhere to go once the story is told. Scroll back up into the section to see the finished text grey out and clear.

Version A holds the page on the section: every scroll gesture types the next sentence, keyboard arrows and space work too, and once the story is told the next gesture lets the page move on. Scrolling up is never held. Version B never holds the page: it types each sentence as you scroll past its place, so a hard flick types two or three in quick succession and a small one types none.

On phones version A works the same way with swipes: one swipe, one sentence. Version B leaves touch scrolling native.
